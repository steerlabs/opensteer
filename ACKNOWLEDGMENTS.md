# Acknowledgments

Opensteer benefits from the work of many open-source communities.
[T3 Code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) shaped much of Opensteer: our agent
orchestration design is adapted from T3 Code's, and it influenced our contributor
workflow and desktop CLI integration. Thank you to the T3 team.
[yabai](https://github.com/koekeishiya/yabai) provides a public reference for macOS
window-focus event ordering. [Cua](https://github.com/trycua/cua) informed parts of
macOS app observation. We thank these projects and their contributors.

required license notices.

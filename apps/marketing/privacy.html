Your computer runs the work

Opensteer runs on your own computer. The iPhone and iPad app connects to that computer so you can give agents instructions, follow their replies and respond to requests. Your computer stores your Opensteer work, including conversations, project data and connected-service configuration.

Our Account and connection services help you sign in, find your connected computers, reach them and receive notifications. We do not provide a cloud-hosted computer or agent workspace.

Agents use the AI services you configure. Instructions, attachments and information an agent reads can go to the AI service used for that task. That service's terms and privacy practices govern its processing, including retention and any use of data to improve models. Keeping Opensteer on your own computer does not prevent the AI service from receiving task data.

Information our services process

When you use an Opensteer Account, our Account service stores information needed to identify and authenticate you. This includes your name, email address, account and sign-in-provider identifiers, session records and authorization records. Session records can include your IP address and user-agent information. Provider profile image URLs and access, refresh or ID tokens may also be stored. OAuth access and refresh tokens use encrypted storage.

We offer Sign in with Apple and Google. Your provider supplies identity information needed to create or sign in to your Opensteer Account. If you use Apple's private email relay, we receive the relay address Apple supplies. We do not receive your Apple or Google account password through this sign-in flow.

The Account service keeps a directory of linked computers and information needed to reach and verify them, such as their names, identifiers, addresses, public-key identities, connection state and account association. Connection and authorization records help prevent unauthorized access and complete connection setup. Optional connected-service authorization can pass through our OAuth handoff service, which processes the authorization exchange and stores temporary encrypted handoff information and status. The broker processes provider token exchanges transiently and seals handoff results for the destination computer. Cleanup clears expired encrypted context and handoff payloads; acknowledgment clears delivered payloads. Status and spent-link diagnostic records can remain.

If you contact support, we receive the information you send us, including your email address and message. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or unnecessary private work. The Account service disables automatic request-line logging, but records operational errors, push delivery outcomes and cleanup failures. Authentication sessions can retain IP addresses and user-agent information. Infrastructure providers may process connection metadata to operate and secure their services.

Information on your phone

The app keeps connection settings and credentials needed to reconnect. It can also retain drafts, chosen attachment copies and saved conversation/task views. Saved views use an encrypted local cache; iOS may clear cache files. Credentials and device keys use native secure storage. Signing out or removing a connection clears relevant local authorization and saved-view keys. Data already sent to your computer or an AI provider is separate from this local storage.

You can choose photos or files to attach to a conversation. The app uploads selected attachments to your Opensteer computer, including while preparing a message. This attachment feature is separate from the optional Photos metadata access described below.

Optional access to iPhone data

You can add an iPhone to your Opensteer installation and choose which supported data agents may use while Opensteer is open on that iPhone. Your choices in Opensteer and iOS permissions both apply. You can decline access, use limited access where iOS supports it, lower an agent's access or turn sharing off.

Calendar
Calendars and events. With read-and-change access, agents may add, change or delete events.
Contacts
Contact names, organizations, email addresses, phone numbers and birthdays. Access is read only and can be limited to selected contacts.
Reminders
Reminder lists and reminders. With read-and-change access, agents may add, change, complete or delete reminders.
Location
A current reading with its accuracy. Precise or approximate location depends on your iOS setting. This integration does not provide continuous location history.
Photos
Read-only metadata, including dates, dimensions, media types, duration, favorites and album names. This integration does not return photo/video image bytes, filenames, EXIF information or photo coordinates. Limited access restricts it to selected items.

What agents see goes to your Opensteer computer and the AI service the task uses. It can become part of the task's conversation history and the AI provider's processing. Consider whether a task needs information about other people before enabling access to contacts, events or reminders.

Only your action starts permission prompts or approves new access. Turning sharing off stops new access; it does not retrieve information agents have already read. You can also change system permissions in iPhone Settings.

Microphone and speech

If you choose supported on-device dictation, Apple's on-device speech recognizer converts audio into draft text. Dictation audio stays in native memory; the app sends the words to your computer when you send the message.

Live voice uses OpenAI through Codex and your signed-in ChatGPT account on your computer. Starting a call requires microphone access and sends live audio to that service. Voice text and task context can become part of conversation history on your computer; Codex also keeps local diagnostic logs. OpenAI’s terms and privacy practices govern its audio and transcript processing and retention.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and depend on your device settings and Account connection. Our Account service stores a push token associated with your app installation, platform, preferences and Account so it can ask Apple Push Notification service to deliver an alert. We also keep routing identifiers and delivery records, such as task/thread identifiers, event type, timestamps, delivery status and retry information.

Your computer can encrypt a notification's title and message for your device before sending it through the Account service and Apple. The Account service receives the encrypted preview without its decryption key. Your device decrypts it; otherwise a generic alert can appear. Your lock-screen notification settings determine what someone near your phone may see. Turning notifications off does not retract an alert already delivered.

Purposes and sharing

We use the Account, connection and notification information to provide those functions, authenticate access and secure the service. We use support communications to respond to your request.

Providers that operate our infrastructure, handle sign-in or deliver notifications process information needed for those functions. The Account deployment configuration uses Google Cloud infrastructure and Cloud SQL in the us-west1 region. Cloudflare provides Connect tunnels and routing. Apple and Google provide sign-in; Apple delivers iOS push notifications. The website is hosted on Cloudflare. When configured, the website uses PostHog for page views and interaction analytics, including its session-recording features, and Crisp for optional support chat. Those website features can process browser identifiers, page and interaction data, or information you send to support. These website services are separate from the iPhone app.

Your chosen AI and connected-service providers also process information you instruct agents to use. Their policies apply to that processing. When another person operates the Opensteer server you connect to, that operator can control its data and retention; contact them about their practices.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

We may disclose information where the law requires it, to protect rights or safety, or in a business transfer, subject to applicable law.

Google Workspace data

If you authorize an agent to use Google Workspace, it may access the data and perform the actions you approved. Work and connected-service credentials belong to your Opensteer computer; authorization handoff may use our broker as described above.

We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Opensteer's use and transfer of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Our services retain Account, connection, authorization and notification records for the time needed to operate those functions and meet applicable security and legal requirements. Expired authorization and connection operations are cleaned up after their validity or recovery windows. OAuth handoff cleanup removes encrypted payloads rather than necessarily deleting diagnostic status rows. Notification previews are cleared on terminal delivery, expiry or device disablement; delivery metadata can remain. Account and device records remain while needed for their active functions or until removed. Operational and support records may remain while needed to resolve failures or requests and protect the service. Deletion from the active Account database is separate from any infrastructure backups.

You can sign out, remove connections and revoke optional phone permissions. Signing out does not delete your Account. To request access, correction or deletion of information TrendUp AI holds, contact tim@opensteer.com.

When signed in to your Opensteer Account, open Settings, then Account, and choose “Delete Account”. This opens the Account deletion page, where you may need to sign in again before confirming “Delete Account”. Deletion turns off Connect for owned computers and removes the central Account, authentication and owned computer-directory records; it does not erase databases on your computer. Manage that work on your computer, and use each AI/connected-service provider's controls for data it holds. The service attempts to revoke the stored Apple sign-in refresh token after Account deletion. If Apple revocation fails, Account deletion still completes; you can also remove Opensteer under Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings. Account deletion cascades to associated push devices, deliveries and OAuth broker records.

Security and international processing

Opensteer uses authenticated connections, encryption and native credential storage to protect information. No security measure eliminates every risk. Keep your computer, phone and connected AI accounts secure.

Providers may process data outside your country. Contact us with questions about processing locations or privacy rights, including access, correction and deletion requests available under applicable law.

Children

Opensteer is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to use it. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you need permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Connect a device

    Install Opensteer on your Mac, or get an Opensteer Cloud Computer. That becomes your Home: where your browser profiles, apps, plugins, tasks and rules live. Add your phone too.

  2. 2

    Connect an agent, and choose

    Add Opensteer to ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or Codex. It signs in the ordinary way, and you pick what it may use and how often it has to ask.

  3. 3

    Watch and stop from your phone

    See each thing it does as it happens. Answer when it asks. Stop it with one tap, wherever you are.

You decide what each agent may use.

One browser profile, one app, one plugin, or everything. And how often it has to ask.

  • A browser profile of its own

    Start it in a new profile that is signed out, or share one you're already signed in to. Opensteer tells you plainly what sharing means.

  • A second agent starts with nothing

    It sees only what you give it. The first agent's work stays its own.

  • It asks before it changes something

    In Supervised mode each change waits for you: Allow once, or Don't allow. Allowing once never gives it more access.

  • Passwords and cards stay with Opensteer

    When a page needs one, the agent asks, you allow it, and Opensteer fills it in on your Mac. It is not sent to the agent, but an agent working in that page can read it.

What it won't do.

Better to know now than to find out halfway through a task.

  • It can't undo. Opensteer can stop what happens next, but it can't undo what an agent already did.

  • It doesn't hide the agent. Opensteer doesn't disguise automation from websites, and it doesn't solve CAPTCHAs for an agent. If a site wants a check, you can do it yourself in the page.

  • A sleeping Mac can't help. Agents can't use your Mac while it is asleep or offline. An Opensteer Cloud Computer stays on.

  • Filled is not hidden. Saved passwords and cards are filled in the browser on your own Mac, not on other devices, and are not sent to the agent. An agent working in that page can still read what is on it.

Set up your computer first

Opensteer runs on your own computer. The iPhone app connects to it; it requires a running Opensteer computer. Start Opensteer on that computer, then follow the connection setup shown in the app. Your agents use the accounts and tools configured on your computer. Download Opensteer for Mac.

If your phone cannot connect

Check that your computer is awake, online and running Opensteer. For a local connection, put both devices on the same network and check the connection address. For remote access, check that your computer is linked to your Opensteer Account and Connect is enabled. Confirm that your phone is using the intended Account and computer, then try connecting again.

Privacy and Account deletion

Read our Privacy Policy for information about phone permissions, AI providers and data storage. You can revoke optional access in iPhone Settings and turn phone sharing off in Opensteer.

To delete your Opensteer Account, open Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. Sign in again if asked and confirm deletion on the Account page. This disables Connect for your owned computers and deletes central Account records; it does not erase work stored on your computer or by AI providers. Signing out alone does not delete your Account. Email tim@opensteer.com for privacy requests or if you cannot access the deletion flow.

Report a bug

Report public bugs at github.com/steerlabs/opensteer/issues. Issues are public, so send private Account and privacy requests by email.

Report a security issue

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, not in a public issue. Our security policy says what to include.

The old Opensteer

We shut down the old Opensteer command-line tool, agent templates and web console, and rebuilt Opensteer as an app for your Mac and phone. If you have questions about the old version or need help moving off it, email tim@opensteer.com.

Set up your Home first

Opensteer keeps your browser profiles, apps, plugins, tasks and rules in one Home. Your Home runs on your own Mac or on an Opensteer Cloud Computer, and the phone app connects to it. Start Opensteer on your Mac, then follow the connection setup shown in the app. Download Opensteer for Mac.

If your phone cannot connect

Check that your Mac is awake, online and running Opensteer. For a local connection, put both devices on the same network and check the connection address. For remote access, check that your Mac is linked to your Opensteer account and Remote access is on. Confirm that your phone is using the intended account and Home, then try connecting again.

Connect or disconnect an agent

In Opensteer, open Settings, then Connected agents, choose Add next to your agent and follow its steps. You choose what it may use when it asks to connect. Agents such as ChatGPT and Claude run in the cloud, so on a Mac Home turn on Remote access first; they can reach your Mac only while it is on and awake.

To stop an agent's work, press Stop in its task. To take away everything you gave it, open it in Connected agents and choose Disconnect. Opensteer can stop what happens next, but it can't undo what an agent already did. If it says Uncertain, check the page or app the agent was using.

Opensteer Cloud Computer

Set up, suspend or delete your Cloud Computer in Opensteer under Settings, then Account. It starts as a new Home, so connected agents connect again to its new address. Opensteer bills you through Stripe each month, and you can cancel any time. For billing questions, email tim@opensteer.com.

Privacy and account deletion

Read our Privacy Policy for information about phone permissions, AI providers and data storage. You can revoke optional access in iPhone Settings and turn phone sharing off in Opensteer.

To delete your Opensteer account, open Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. Sign in again if asked and confirm deletion on the Account page. This turns off Remote access for the computers you own and deletes central account records; it does not erase work stored on your computer or by AI providers. Signing out alone does not delete your account. Email tim@opensteer.com for privacy requests or if you cannot access the deletion flow.

Report a bug

Report public bugs at github.com/steerlabs/opensteer/issues. Issues are public, so send private account and privacy requests by email.

Report a security issue

Report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, not in a public issue. Our security policy says what to include.

The old Opensteer

We shut down the old Opensteer command-line tool, agent templates and web console, and rebuilt Opensteer for your Mac, your phone and the AI agents you already use. If you have questions about the old version or need help moving off it, email tim@opensteer.com.
